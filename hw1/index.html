Introduction to Computer Networking

Computer networking allows devices such as computers, smartphones, and servers to communicate and share resources. Networks can range from small home networks to large systems that connect millions of devices across the world.

Networks are an essential part of modern technology. They allow people to access websites, exchange emails, share files, and use online services. Without computer networking, many of the technologies we rely on every day would not be possible.

How Do Networks Work?

Computer networks use hardware devices and communication protocols to transfer data between connected systems. Routers, switches, and wireless access points help devices connect, while protocols establish the rules for exchanging information.
